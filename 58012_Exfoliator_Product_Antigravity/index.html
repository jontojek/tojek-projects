PIPELINE STANDARDS
AXIS ONE STANDARD

Sleeve Specular Kill

The outer squeeze tube plastic must read as 100% matte with zero specular reflections, gloss, or vertical commercial highlight bars on the curved container boundaries.

HARD PIPELINE CONSTRAINT

SYSTEM INSTRUCTIONS DIRECTIVES

SYSTEM INSTRUCTION FOR MATTE REFLECTION: - Outer tube plastic is 100% matte. Specular roughness = 1.0, glossiness = 0.0, reflectivity = 0.0. - Convert all lighting to flat ambient. The container surface must read as an unreflective, rough frosted sleeve with absolutely zero vertical white highlights or environment reflections on curved edges.

PHOTOSHOP COMP COMPLIANCE

Retouching must use the raw matte color map as an unlit diffuse card, completely suppressing specular highlights inside Photoshop blend layers, ensuring full manual control of plastic specular in post.

CGI SHADER REBUILD RULE

In Maya/Blender, set outer plastic shader transmission roughness = 0.95, specular IOR = 1.33, and assign zero metallic highlights on the cylinder's volume boundaries.
