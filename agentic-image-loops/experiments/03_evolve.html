experiment 03

Evolve

A small genetic algorithm where the agent is the selection pressure. Four images per generation - different prompt tweaks, different seeds - and the best two get bred: one kept unchanged, two mutated, one crossover. In the first generation, a plain seed reroll matched the hill-climb champion, confirming that its persistent hood defect came from the seed, not the prompt. The highest-scoring image came from describing the paint material itself rather than the reflections it should produce.
